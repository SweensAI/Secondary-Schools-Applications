Getting ready for the tests

Working backwards from each exam date. Content overlaps heavily across all the tests — verbal and non-verbal reasoning, maths and English comprehension — so practice for one helps the others.

Good news: since Jacob is only in Year 5, there's no rush. His Redbridge 11+ sitting is in September 2027, not this year — that's over a year away. This year is for visiting schools informally and getting a feel for fit; formal preparation doesn't need to start in earnest until Year 6.

Bursaries & scholarships

All three independents — Chigwell, Forest and Bancroft's — offer financial help, but bursaries and scholarships work differently and it's easy to conflate them.

Bursaries — means-tested

Based on household income and assets, not exam performance. You disclose finances (often via a third-party assessor); awards can cover a small proportion up to 100% of fees. Reassessed roughly annually as circumstances change, and usually applied for separately from — and often earlier than — the entrance exam registration.

Scholarships — merit-based

Awarded for academic, music, sport, art or drama achievement, judged through the entrance exam itself, a separate test, or an audition/trial. They carry prestige and recognition, but typically only a modest fee discount (often 10–20%, sometimes none at all for pure academic awards) — rarely enough alone to make a place affordable. Many families combine a scholarship with a means-tested bursary top-up.

Chigwell School

Independent · Years 7–13
Bursaries
Means-tested, available at 11+, 13+ and 16+ entry, up to 100% of tuition. Assessed by an external agency, Bursary Assessment Associates, based on full household financial disclosure.
Scholarships
Academic, Sport and Music scholarships offered at 11+ and 13+ (Art & Drama added at 16+). Judged via exam performance and, for Music, audition.
Timing & how to apply
The financial (bursary) assessment runs on its own, earlier timeline than the general 11+ registration — for the most recent cycle this opened in early November with a mid-December deadline, roughly two months before the January entrance exam. Apply for the bursary assessment and register for the entrance exam separately; contact admissions to align the two.

Forest School

Independent · Years 7–13
Bursaries
Means-tested support and hardship funds exist, but these are more limited in scope than at Chigwell or Bancroft's — historically including a small number of places reserved for families facing a sudden, significant drop in income (e.g. an existing family, not primarily a broad new-entrant scheme). Worth asking admissions directly how many new-entry bursary places are available for the 2027 cohort.
Scholarships
Academic, Music, Art, Sport and Drama scholarships available, typically a modest fee reduction rather than a large remission.
Other reductions
A flat 5% sibling or clergy discount is available, separate from means-tested support.
Timing & how to apply
Register interest in financial assistance alongside — or just after — the 18 November 2026 entrance registration deadline; ask admissions for the current bursary application form and its deadline, as this isn't always published on the same schedule as the exam.

Bancroft's School

Independent · Years 7–13
Bursaries
Means-tested Assisted Places at 11+ and 16+, up to 100% of fees — Bancroft's flagship financial-aid route, reassessed annually as family circumstances change. This is the most substantial of the three schemes in scope, though the number of places is limited relative to ~450–500 candidates sitting the entrance test each year.
Scholarships
Academic Scholarships carry prestige and recognition but no fee remission on their own. Music Scholarships cover tuition costs in one instrument, not general school fees.
Timing & how to apply
Indicate interest in an Assisted Place when registering for the 11+ by 1 December 2026; a full means assessment follows for shortlisted families, typically alongside or shortly after the January assessment day, ahead of February offers.
Practical tip: means-tested bursary applications often need financial disclosure prepared well in advance (tax returns, payslips, assets). If cost is a deciding factor in whether to pursue any of these three schools, it's worth contacting each school's bursar or admissions office now, before the entrance exam registration deadlines, to understand what's realistically available for the 2027 entry year — rather than finding out about the aid on offer only after an exam pass.
